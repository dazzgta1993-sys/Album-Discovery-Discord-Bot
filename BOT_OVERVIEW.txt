PROJECT SUMMARY:
A high-fidelity, studio-grade Discord Music Bot & Real-Time Command Center Hub 
built upon the "Album Discovery" music intelligence engine. 

Unlike traditional bots that blindly scrape arbitrary YouTube search queries,
this system unites 9 multi-platform music engines, real-time editorial metadata,
lossless studio taxonomy, an autonomous AI Autoplay Vibe DJ, an advanced Triple-Deck 
audio pipeline with two-step zero-buffer preloading, an interactive Mastering Scope 
& Acoustic Audio Lab, dynamic YouTube channel & artist feed auto-polling, and a 
live web Command Center at http://localhost:8905.

WHAT MAKES THIS BOT UNIQUE (COMPETITIVE DIFFERENTIATORS)
================================================================================

1. 🎛️ Continuous Triple-Deck Audio Pipeline (Deck 1 Live | Deck 2 Ready | Deck 3 Cued):
   - Traditional bots suffer from 2-5 second silent buffering gaps between songs.
   - If someone skips twice quickly, traditional bots stall and crash.
   - This bot runs a continuous 3-deck pipeline: Deck 1 streams live, Deck 2 is pre-warmed 
     with pre-resolved direct audio stream URLs for instant transitions, and Deck 3 is pre-cued 
     in the chute two steps ahead with full double-skip protection.

2. 🎚️ Mastering Scope & Acoustic Audio Lab (Real-Time DSP Telemetry):
   - Full 48-band 32-bit float FFT frequency spectrum canvas with responsive peak-hold physics.
   - Dual Stereo VU meters with real-time dBFS readings and -0.5 dB True-Peak limiter ceiling markers.
   - 8 live pipeline telemetry metrics: Resampler Engine, Headroom Guard, Stream Buffer Flow,
     Bitstream Codec, Dynamic Crest Factor, Headroom Margin, High-Freq Air Ceiling, and Clipping Events.
   - Active True-Peak clipping detection & real-time crimson alert warning (⚠️ N clipping events detected).
   - Interactive 1-click DSP Self-Check diagnostic suite.

3. 🔄 Autonomous Channel Poller & Dynamic Feed Subscriptions (My Crate):
   - Background channel poller (channel_poller.py) automatically monitors subscribed YouTube 
     uploaders and artists (e.g. Orryy.) every 2 hours with exponential backoff.
   - Zero hardcoding: completely customizable feed subscriptions persisted to SQLite WAL.
   - Automatically synchronizes newly uploaded tracks directly into dedicated playlists in My Crate.
   - Instant "Follow Feed", "Sync", and "Unsubscribe" actions from the Web Control Hub.

4. 🚀 Zero-Jitter Live Stream Pipeline & True-Peak Ceiling:
   - Direct real-time packet streaming eliminates speed-warping ("slow then fast"), pitch drift,
     buffer underruns, and DC-offset pops.
   - Dedicated -0.45 dB True-Peak Headroom Guard (volume=0.95) prevents inter-sample peaks from 
     clipping on Discord's native Opus 48kHz stereo encoder.

5. 🧠 Multi-Platform Curated Autoplay DJ & Vibe Anchor:
   - Traditional autoplay simply scrapes YouTube's algorithm and quickly drifts into 
     viral TikTok memes, amateur bedroom recordings, or unrelated genres.
   - This bot features a multi-tiered recommendation engine prioritizing Deezer 
     Curated Artist Radio and YouTube Music studio masters.
   - Sequential Lookahead Chain: Deck 3 is seeded directly from Deck 2, creating a smooth, 
     professionally curated musical story arc.
   - Vibe Anchor Protection & 1-Click Lock: Retains the user's seed artist and genre 
     across skips, with an interactive ⚓ Lock pill to lock the DJ into a specific sound.
   - Anti-Junk Shield: Actively filters out hoodtrap, type beats, TikTok remixes, 
     karaoke, skits (<65s), and low-listen amateur spam.

6. 🎧 Accurate Playback, Smart Queueing & Pause-Offset Synchronization:
   - Tracks real-time elapsed seconds with precise pause-duration accounting 
     (time.time() - play_start_time - total_paused_time).
   - Smart Queue Guard: /play automatically adds tracks to the queue when music is already 
     playing (including during AI Autoplay transitions) without cutting off the current song, 
     while /playnow immediately interrupts and plays right now.
   - Ensures visual seeker bars, timestamp indicators, and dashboard telemetry stay 
     closely synchronized with Discord voice audio across pause and resume events.

7. 🧬 Real-Time Session Music DNA & Acoustic Spectrum Visualizer:
   - 100% Dynamic Public Taxonomy Resolution: Classifies tracks across 10 core musical genres and 
     30+ subgenres via live MusicBrainz and Last.fm taxonomy graphs without static artist limits.
   - Heuristic Session Metrics: Estimates session Energy (0-100), Danceability (0-100), and 
     Electronic-to-Acoustic sonic ratios.
   - Multi-Color Spectrum Capsule: Proportional visual bar displaying exact session genre distribution.
   - Top DNA Influences: Maps contributing artists with tailored subgenre badges.
   - Thumbs Up / Down Training: Interactive feedback system persisting to SQLite taste profiles.

8. 🎙️ Native Discord Voice Channel Status Synchronization:
   - Automatically sets Discord's native voice channel status bar to the currently playing 
     song ("🎶 Artist - Title") without creating distracting spam messages in text channels.
   - Automatically clears when playback stops or the queue completes.

9. 🌐 Unified 9-Platform Intelligence Ecosystem:
   - Seamlessly blends Deezer Studio Masters, YouTube Music, YouTube Live/Concerts, 
     Spotify catalogs, Apple Music / iTunes high-res covers, SoundCloud DJ mixes, 
     MusicBrainz archival discographies, and real-time Official UK/US Billboard charts.

10. 🖥️ Studio Command Center Web Hub (http://localhost:8905) & Full 2-Way Sync:
    - Every action in Discord (/dj, /volume, /quality, /loop, /shuffle, or embed buttons) and

1. UNIFIED 9-ENGINE MUSIC ECOSYSTEM
================================================================================

Both the Web Command Center and Discord slash commands (/play, /playnow, /dj, /search)
support 9 distinct intelligence & streaming engines:

1. 🌐 Multi-Engine Auto (Smart Hybrid Cascade)
   - Blends signals across all engines dynamically.
   - Cascades from artist radio -> collaborators -> studio catalog -> charts.

2. 🔴 YouTube Music Radio (ytmusic)
   - Official studio-quality audio streams (Topic channels).
   - Powered by Google/YouTube Music's song-to-song recommendation algorithm.

3. ▶️ YouTube Video & Live (youtube)
   - Searches music videos, live concert recordings, and acoustic sessions.
   - Ideal for live versions not found on studio albums.

4. 🟣 Deezer Studio Masters (deezer)
   - Official studio catalog metadata, artist radio, and collaborator graphs.
   - Deep artist relationship graphs, official artist radio, and verified collaborator trees.

5. 🟢 Spotify Catalog Match (spotify)
   - Resolves direct Spotify track, album, and playlist URLs.
   - Searches mainstream curated hit repertoire and top catalog hits.

6. 🍎 Apple Music / iTunes (apple)
   - Official Apple Music / iTunes metadata, lossless studio tracks, and ratings.
   - High-resolution 1400x1400 cover art resolution.

7. 🟠 SoundCloud (soundcloud)
   - DJ sets, club remixes, underground tracks, unreleased demos, and bootlegs.
   - Direct SoundCloud URL resolution for tracks, sets, and playlists.

8. 🏛️ MusicBrainz Archive (musicbrainz)
   - MetaBrainz Foundation open music encyclopedia.
   - Canonical studio LP release-group taxonomy, disambiguation, and archival discographies.

9. 📊 Official Charts Company (charts)
   - Live verified real-world radio broadcast and sales charts (UK Top 40, US Billboard Hot 100).
   - Functions as an anchor engine to keep sessions energetic and recognizable.

3. CORE ARCHITECTURE & AUDIO ENGINE
===============================================================================

1. Triple-Deck Audio Pipeline (player.py):
   - FFmpeg with high-bitrate Opus stereo encoding (48kHz).
   - 3-Deck Continuous Preloading:
     * Deck 1 (Live): Actively streaming to the Discord voice channel.
     * Deck 2 (Next Up): Buffers and holds the stream for the immediate next song.
     * Deck 3 (In The Chute): Resolves metadata and stream tokens 2 tracks ahead.
   - Source-Bound Callback Protection: Prevents delayed FFmpeg process cleanup from 
     clearing active playback flags during zero-gap transitions.
   - Double-Skip & Network Latency Protection: Even if members skip twice in rapid succession,
     playback begins instantly without buffering or waiting on API round-trips.
   - Dynamic Volume Transformer: Scales volume smoothly without resetting stream sockets.
   - Headroom Guard (-0.45 dB / volume=0.95): Guarantees zero clipping events on Opus compression.
   - Configurable Target Bitrate: Auto (best available source stream), 128k, 192k, or 320k FFmpeg target encoding.

2. Autonomous AI Vibe Engine (vibe_engine.py):
   - Prioritizes Deezer Curated Radio & Related Artist Graphs over uncurated feeds.
   - Lookahead Seeding: Deck 3 is seeded by Deck 2 for seamless musical progression.
   - Comprehensive Blacklisting: Cross-checks history, current track, Deck 2, Deck 3, and user queue.
   - Evaluates vibe worthiness: blocks low-listen amateur tracks, skits (<65s), 
     multi-hour podcasts (>1500s), karaoke, sped-up/slowed spam, hoodtrap, and type beats.
   - Intelligent Anchor System: Preserves seed artist across autoplay skips with interactive lock.
   - Anti-Repeat Radio Cooldown: Remembers recent tracks to prevent artist or title spam.

3. Background Channel Poller (channel_poller.py):
   - Monitors subscribed YouTube channels, playlists, and artists every 2 hours.
   - Extracts newly uploaded tracks via yt-dlp metadata flat-playlist extraction.
   - Automatically populates custom crate playlists without interrupting active playback.

4. Persistent SQLite WAL Database (store.py):
   - Location: data/cache.db
   - guild_settings: Non-destructively merges and persists volume, quality, loop mode, 
     autoplay, anchor lock, vibe mode, drift frequency, hit depth, cooldown, era filter, 
     explicit shield, energy dial, and engine source per guild.
   - crate_subscriptions: Persists followed feed channels, display titles, and last check timestamps.
   - playback_history: Records played tracks with guild ID, metadata, and timestamps (plus instant clear).
   - api_cache & stream_resolutions: Caches MusicBrainz, Deezer, Spotify, and stream lookups with TTL.

5. Title Sanitizer & Normalizer (normalizer.py & taxonomy.py):
   - Cleans remastered, deluxe, re-issue, spatial audio, and junk tags to ensure 
     clean studio audio matching.

4. DISCORD SLASH COMMANDS (25 Active Commands)
===============================================================================

Playback & Audio Controls:
- /play <query> [source]        : Plays immediately if idle, or adds to the queue if music is playing.
- /playnow <query> [source]     : Immediately plays a track right now (bypasses queue).
- /skip                         : Skips current track to the next queued or preloaded item.
- /pause                        : Pauses current playback.
- /resume                       : Resumes paused playback.
- /stop                         : Stops playback, clears queue, and disconnects from voice.
- /queue                        : Displays paginated queue with runtime stats.
- /nowplaying                   : Displays rich embed with interactive control buttons (including ❤️ Save to Crate).
- /volume <1-100>               : Sets volume percentage in real time (synced with Web Hub).
- /quality <auto|128k|192k|320k>: Switches streaming bitrate dynamically (synced with Web Hub).
- /shuffle                      : Shuffles the queue (or re-rolls Deck 2 & Deck 3 AI tracks).
- /loop <off|track|queue>       : Sets loop repeat mode (synced with Web Hub).
- /history                      : Shows the last 10 tracks played in this server.
- /favorite [playlist]          : Saves the currently playing track to My Crate (Favorites or custom playlist).
- /crate [action] [playlist]    : View, Play All, or Shuffle & Play your saved Favorites or custom playlists.

AI DJ & Vibe Intelligence:
- /dj [engine]                  : Toggles Autoplay DJ or locks it to one of the 9 engines.
- /brain                        : Displays live telemetry embed of active engine, anchor, 
                                  drift frequency, explicit shield, and last decision.

Music Discovery & Discography:
- /search <query> [source]      : Interactive dropdown search across all 9 engines to queue tracks.
- /album <query>                : Canonical album card with interactive tracklist & "Queue Entire Album".
- /artist <name>                : Artist profile with fan count, studio albums, and top hits.
- /discography <artist>         : Categorized discography browser (Studio LPs, EPs, Live, Compilations).
- /newreleases [limit]          : Fresh weekly editorial drops and studio releases.
- /timemachine <year>           : Explores landmark albums from 1960 through 2026.
- /charts [country] [limit]     : Queues live UK Top 40, US Billboard, or Global chart tracks.
- /radio                        : Streams from 94 curated cloud genre and vibe stations.
